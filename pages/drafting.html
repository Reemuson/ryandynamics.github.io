Shape concepts into drawings.

We deliver clean, standards-compliant CAD models and manufacturing drawings you can trust. Clear views, correct callouts, and documentation that moves smoothly from design to fabrication.

From reverse-engineering existing assemblies to new product concepts, we create robust, parametric models and drawings ready for change.

Deliverables include GD&T drawings, DFM/DFT notes, sheet-metal flat patterns with bend tables, and weldment cut lists with weld callouts.

  • Manufacturing-Ready

  • Sheet Metal & Weldments

    Flat patterns & bend tables, cut lists & weld callouts.
