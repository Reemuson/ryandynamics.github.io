Connect designs with electronics.

We design the electronics that power your products—from robust circuits and PCBs to the wiring that ties it all together. Our focus is performance, reliability, manufacturability, and clear documentation for hand-off or production.

Alongside PCB/circuit design (schematics, layout, prototyping), we provide full low-voltage electrical design: wiring harnesses and looms, connector assemblies, pinout definitions, cable schedules, and assembly drawings. We handle connector selection, wire gauge and protection, shielding/EMC, strain relief, labeling, and test plans—suited to automotive, industrial, agriculture, and robotics applications.

From schematic capture to PCB layout, harness drafting, and prototype builds, we manage each stage with precision and attention to detail. Covering DFM/DFT, BOMs, and bring-up testing, so your idea moves smoothly into production.

Why our electronics & wiring make the difference

  • Integrated PCB + Harness

    Boards, looms, and connectorised assemblies designed together for reliability and EMI.

  • Prototype to Production

    One-offs to small runs with DFM/DFT, cable schedules, pinouts, and build docs.

  • Field-Ready Details

    Correct gauge & protection, strain relief, labeling, and test plans for harsh use.
