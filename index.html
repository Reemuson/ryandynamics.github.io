Problems Ryan Dynamics can help solve

Some projects begin with a developed specification. Others begin with a damaged part, incomplete documentation or equipment that needs a new function.

  • An obsolete or damaged electronic, wiring or mechanical part needs to be replaced.
  • Existing equipment needs a custom interface, controller, sensor or adapter.
  • A concept needs to be developed into electronics, firmware, CAD or a working prototype.
  • A physical component needs to be measured, documented, reproduced or modified.
  • Boards, wiring and mechanical components need to work together as a complete assembly.

Selected work

  • Replacement harnesses produced from a damaged sample

    A geophysical survey equipment client required six replacement connector harnesses for a mobile charging station. Working from a damaged sample, the harnesses were documented, assembled and electrically checked within an overnight turnaround. The client confirmed that the replacements operated correctly.
